MDBot for Her

An AI assistant that listens first.

I founded MDBot as a volunteer-based AI assistant that supports parents and children with guidance on growth, learning and care. Its emotionally intelligent version for women empowerment is powered by AWS, and I presented it at AWS Community Day Delhi 2025.

  1. 1

    She speaks or types

    A natural conversation starts the session.

  2. 2

    Amazon Comprehend reads the emotion

    The bot understands how the person feels.

  3. 3

    Amazon Bedrock writes the reply

    The response is shaped by that emotion.

  4. 4

    Amazon Polly speaks it back

    A voice answer makes it feel human.

A dashboard then tracks emotional growth over time.
