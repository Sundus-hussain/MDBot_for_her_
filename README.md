# Sundus Hussain website

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step.

## Before you publish
1. Add your photos to `images/` (see `images/README.txt`).
2. Open `script.js` and set `CONFIG.email` to your email address so the volunteer and enroll form opens an email to you. Leave it empty to send visitors to your LinkedIn instead.

## Host on Cloudflare Pages
1. Sign in at dash.cloudflare.com and open Workers & Pages.
2. Choose Create, then Pages, then Upload assets.
3. Name the project and drag in this whole folder (or the zip, unzipped).
4. Select Deploy. Your site is live at `your-project.pages.dev`.
5. To use your own domain, open the project, choose Custom domains and follow the steps.

To update the site later, open the project and upload the folder again as a new deployment.
